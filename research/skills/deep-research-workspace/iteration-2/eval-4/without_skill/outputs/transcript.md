## Limitations of This Approach vs. Skill-Assisted Research

1. **No current data:** Training data has a cutoff; rapidly evolving statistics (model pricing, adoption percentages, vendor market share) may be stale.
2. **No primary source verification:** Claims are synthesized from memory, not verified against current primary documents.
3. **No citation precision:** Source references are from memory; exact publication dates, sample sizes, and figures cannot be precisely cited.
4. **No gap detection:** A skill with web search can identify emerging themes not well-represented in training data (e.g., new regulatory guidance, recent vendor announcements).
5. **No parallel research:** A multi-agent skill can research multiple sub-topics simultaneously and synthesize findings; this report was produced serially by a single model.
6. **Potential knowledge gaps:** Developments after the training cutoff are absent, which may be significant in a fast-moving domain.
